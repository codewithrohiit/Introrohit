# 🚀 Personal Portfolio & Hiring Website

Welcome to the repository of my personal portfolio website! This website is designed to showcase my skills, projects, background, and experience to potential employers, recruiters, and clients.

🔗 **Live Demo:**[https://github.com/codewithrohiit/Introrohit.git]

---

## 📌 Features

- **About Me:** Overview of my background, passion, and goal.
- **Skills & Tech Stack:** Highlighting technologies and tools I work with.
- **Featured Projects:** Showcasing my top projects with live links and repository references.
- **Experience / Education:** Timeline of my academic background and hands-on experience.
- **Contact Form / Hire Me:** Direct way to reach out to me for job opportunities or freelance work.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

---
